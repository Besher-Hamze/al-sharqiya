ABOUT US
Al -Sharqiya Company was established in
1986 in Al Ain, United Arab Emirates,
specializing in the manufacturing of gypsum
and GRC products. The company later
expanded its operations to Abu Dhabi and
Ajman, offering a wide collection of more
than 2,500 unique mold designs. Following
this success, Al Sharqiya entered the paints
sector as both distributor and applicator for
leading brands , under the name Art Colors,
providing high-quality paint and flooring
solutions

Gypsum
Gypsum : We have been supplying gypsum and

government and private projects over the years.
As one of the oldest GRG suppliers in the United
Arab Emirates, we offer a wide range of designs,
including Moroccan, Islamic, modern, classical,
and many other styles.
