About Us

Al Sharqiya Gypsum & GRC Group was established in 1986 in the United Arab Emirates, building a strong presence and extensive experience across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Al Ain, and Ajman.

With decades of experience in the construction and building materials industry, we expanded our expertise nearly 10 years ago into the supply and application of decorative materials and professional painting solutions, establishing multiple branches to serve our growing customer base.

Over the years, we have successfully completed numerous projects covering building painting, flooring systems, and waterproofing solutions for a wide range of commercial, residential, and government properties.

Today, we continue to grow and develop within this field, combining our long-standing industry experience with high-quality products, professional execution, and a commitment to customer satisfaction. Our goal is to deliver our knowledge, expertise, and proven experience to every client and project we undertake.
